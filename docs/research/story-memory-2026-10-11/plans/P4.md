# P4 Contradictions and Needs a look: implementation plan

**Features:** F-16.27, F-16.28, F-16.29 and F-16.30 (`FEATURES.md` §2.16). Phase P4 of the story memory redesign, approved by the author on 2026-10-11 (`../design.md` §2.1, §2.2 (the `finding` row, the four AI writes: *Update my note* is one), §2.3 ("Reasoning": `judge` joins `NO_REASONING_FEATURES`; "Measuring accuracy": judge agreement and underline precision), §2.4 (all of it), §2.5 (absence is scoped), §2.6 ("Data sharing and toggles"), §2.7 (*Book*: *Check the book*; the Needs a look bullet; Dismissed), §2.10, Part 4 (P4 row), Part 5 ("Check the book", the index and session rows); `../author-answers.md` answers 1, 3, 6, 12, 17, 19, 20, 21, 22; decision 4). **Size:** M, about 1.5 weeks, in six slices. **Status:** planned 2026-10-13; nothing built yet.

**Goal:** the app tells the author, quietly, where her book disagrees with itself or with her notes, and what she has left hanging. Code finds candidate pairs under rules C1–C7 exactly as design §2.4's table says; `judge.v1` (fast tier, JSON, reasoning off, at most 10 pairs per call) rules on each pair twice, with the passages swapped, in two separate calls; only two "contradiction" verdicts make a finding. Findings are derived rows that close themselves when their rule no longer produces them, show in Needs a look's **Conflicts** (book against book, book against a note with *Update my note*, beside P1's filed lines and P2a's typed details) and as soft underlines on both quotes (at most 3 per scene, never saved, never at the caret, never in a paragraph with an unread change), with *Go there · This one's right · Not a problem*. *Not a problem* is keyed by (record, slot, value A, scene A, value B, scene B) into the one Dismissed list with Undo. **Loose ends** (local, free) list threads, characters and planned cards left behind the furthest drafted scene, each with *Ask Ms Scribe*. **Check the book** is priced first, judges every uncached candidate (C7 included), has V4 Pro confirm at most 20 pairs both orders called a contradiction, shows "unclear" as unclear, and reports what it checked and what it did not; it costs nothing when the book has not changed.

**Rules that hold in every slice** (P1's, P2a's, P2b's and P3's, carried over):
1. **Everything is behind `storyMemory === 'v2'`.** A v1 project sends, stores and shows exactly what it does today: `continuity.v2`, the Continuity view and `continuity_finding` keep running there unchanged, and the smoke e2e (`e2e/smoke.spec.ts`, v1, which covers F-13.4) is not edited. New channels are `v2` in `CHANNEL_MEMORY`; a v1 call of one is VALIDATION before the handler.
2. **Migrations are additive** (0038+). `migrate.test.ts` checks that every older table keeps its rows and columns.
3. **Each prompt version** (`judge.v1`, P4's only one) is a new file with a golden test, eval cases, token-report rows, its `aiCostNotes.ts` entry (the planned `judge.v1` and `judge.v1:pro` rows move to shipped) and its data-sharing row, with one `npm run eval:ai -- -u` in the slice that ships it; the commit quotes the token-report diff. `judge` joins `NO_REASONING_FEATURES` and `LOWEST_EFFORT_FEATURES` (design §2.3 "Reasoning"), on both tiers.
4. **No live run can happen in the build sessions** (no API key). The live contradictions run (false alarms, judge agreement, underline precision) is built so the author can run it with `OPENROUTER_API_KEY` (slice 6); every slice says so, and the final slice records the owed run in `FEATURES.md` and `CLAUDE.md` (it is F-16.27's merge condition and P4's in design Part 4).
5. **Absence is never a fact** (P2b rule 9, P2b-D9, P3 rule 5 stand). Loose ends ("no step since 7.3"), "secrets nobody learns" and "no scene shows" are scoped lines composed in the renderer alone from `checkedUpTo` and refs main returns; nothing a prompt carries (`judge.v1`, and the question *Ask Ms Scribe* sends) states that something did not happen or that someone does not know. A test per surface (slice 4).
6. **Findings are derived index data** (AI rule 1's exception, `PLAN.md` §2.6): stored apart from the author's text, marked AI-made, removable (*Not a problem*), costed (each judged pair's cost is on the ledger and in ⓘ), rebuildable, never in the manuscript. The only author-text writes P4 adds are *Update my note* (a code-made proposal the author edits and saves, design §2.2's second exception) and the existing *Update my detail* (P2a). *This one's right* writes a Dismissed row, never the manuscript.
7. Tag, entity, knowledge, Organise and To do code is frozen for edit lists. P4 touches none of it; v1's `continuity` feature, prompt and view are untouched.
8. Lint per folder (`npx eslint --max-warnings 0` on `src/shared`, `src/preload`, `src/main`, `src/renderer`, `cloud`, `e2e`, `scripts`); the full `npm run lint` runs out of memory. The full gate suite runs at most twice per slice. Node-heavy commands under `flock /tmp/mythscribe-heavy.lock`.
9. Each slice adds or updates its `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md` bullet, logs its open calls in `QUESTIONS.md` ("P4 slice n"), and notes its progress in the F-16.27–16.30 text. `src/main/ipc/handlers.ts` (over 5,000 lines) only registers channels; the logic lives in a new `src/main/memory/findings/` folder; `readerService.ts` (1,282 lines) only dispatches the two new job kinds to it. `ThisScenePanel.tsx` (1,261 lines) only mounts the new Needs a look groups, which live in `src/renderer/features/memory/needsALook/`.

**Build order:** slice 1 (the findings data, the candidate rules C1–C7, loose ends and the local recompute: data and pure rules, no AI, no UI) → 2 (`judge.v1`, both orders, the verdict cache, background judging on the reader's queue, `memory:findings`) → 3 (Check the book and Needs a look's answers, main side: C7, estimate, run, Pro confirm, report, *Not a problem*, *This one's right*, *Not a flashback?*, *Update my note*, loose-end views, the rebuild sweep, the instant card's contested test, the v2 continuity route) → 4 (Needs a look and Check the book in the panel) → 5 (underlines in the editor, the hover card, the precision question) → 6 (offline replays, the live contradictions run for the author, final verification). ## 0. The shape of it

```
memory:changed / note save / scene move / flag or typed-detail change ─► `findings` job (local, debounced)
   candidateInputs (stored claims, held cards, years, knows, threads, canon note sentences, membership)
   └─► candidates C1–C6 (pure; C7 only on Check the book): rule, record, slot, side A, side B, held?, hidden?
         └─► lifecycle: upsert by key, delete what no rule produces (closes itself), drop Not a problem keys
               verdict_cache hit for both orders ─► open / unclear / fine at no cost
               uncached ─► `judge` job on the reader's queue (background: ≤10 pairs a run, never past the cap)
                     judge.v1 (fast, JSON, reasoning off): batch of ≤10 pairs in order A,B · second call B,A
                     └─► both "c" ─► open finding ─► Needs a look Conflicts (badge) · underlines (≤3 a scene)
Check the book (asked, priced first): + C7 ─► judge every uncached pair-order ─► Pro confirms ≤20 open
   ─► report: found, unclear, checked, not checked, hidden as flashbacks (Not a flashback?), secrets nobody learns
looseEnds (local): threads, characters, planned cards behind the furthest drafted scene ─► Ask Ms Scribe
```

### 1.3 The rules, exactly as design §2.4's table says

Inputs are the active draft's claims in readable scenes (never an Idea scene, an inactive draft or a scene left out of compile: claims never come from them, and the invariants hold it), live or stale, never waiting. "Narration-now" is `raisesAlone`. Values agree when `detailsAgree` says so (equal, or one's words hold the other's: "grey" in "grey-blue").

| Rule | Candidate in code | Sides |
|---|---|---|
| **C1** | Per record, fixed slot and (for kinship) object: the values grouped by agreement (union over `detailsAgree`); each group's representative is its earliest narration-now claim, else its earliest claim. The anchor group is the earliest group with a narration-now representative; each other group is paired with it: k groups, k−1 pairs (P4-D2). No anchor group (no narration-now value at all) → no pair. | two `claim`s |
| **C2** `dead` | A narration-now `alive` claim whose value reads as dead (`deathWord`: dead, died, killed, drowned, slain, murdered, perished, buried, hanged, "passed away"; a closed list) at scene A, and a later scene B (reading order) where the record is on the page (a held `present` entry with its verified quote), with no narration-now `alive` claim reading as living between them. B's held card a verified flashback → `hidden`. Typed POV and *+ also here* never raise it (P4-D3). | `claim` (the death), `presence` |
| **C2** `body` | Lasting body slots (`hair`, `scars`, `BODY_SLOTS`): consecutive narration-now claims of one record, A then B in reading order, whose values disagree, where B is the record's next appearance after A (no scene between them where the record is Here by the stored cards' `present`, the typed POV or *+ also here*; or the same scene), and no claim of that slot from A to B states a change (`change`). Either scene a verified flashback → `hidden`. | two `claim`s |
| **C3** | Per secret and knower X: the earliest counted line (`knowsLine` = `counts`) is `tells` or `refers` at A, and a later counted `LEARNS` line of X (saw, told, overheard, read, deduced) not in `memory` mode exists at B (a later scene, or the same scene's later paragraph). Skipped when `knew_before` (secret, X) exists, when X's earliest counted line is `knewBefore`, or when a group X belongs to (`groupsOf`) has a counted line for the secret at or before A. Never raised without the later learning line (a slip with no learning scene is neither a finding nor a question, design Part 6). | two `claim`s, always two quotes |
| **C4** `birth` | A stated age (`age`, narration-now, parsed by `statedAge`'s number reading) at scene A; the record's birth year as of A (`birthYear`: a `born` claim with one year, or a canon Pinned "Born:" line); A's own year, typed for A or quoted (verified) in A, never carried (`storyYears` source `typed` or `quoted`, design Part 6 "Story years"); both years in the same calendar (the year values' non-digit words equal, P4-D6). Candidate when `|stated − (year − born)| > 1`. A a verified flashback → `hidden`. | `claim` (the age), `claim` (born) or `note` (the Pinned line); `extra.year` |
| **C4** `falls` | Two stated ages of one record in reading order, A then B, neither scene a verified flashback, B's age lower than A's. (A flashback side → `hidden`.) | two `claim`s |
| **C5** | A thread's counted `resolves` step at A and a counted `advances` step at a later scene B (one scene with both reads as resolved, `stepRank`, so the same scene never pairs). | two `claim`s |
| **C6** | Per record: each live claim in a fixed slot or kinship (any voice and mode: the note is the canon side, P4-D1) against the canon note sentences that pair with it (`pairsWith`) and whose value code read (`noteValue`) disagrees; lasting and transient slots only against dated note lines whose scene is the claim's scene (the dated scene resolved by its title, then its number; ambiguous → skipped). Canon notes are a record's Pinned paragraphs and canon Research pages (written `note` and uploaded `research` rows of `memory_passage_fts` with canon `canon`; never Ideas, Not canon pages or paragraphs, scene notes or synopses). At most 2 note sentences per claim (its own sheet's first, then by page order); at most 10 C6 pairs judged per reading (the judge's per-run limit, §2.3). The code part also runs when a note is saved (§1.5). | `claim`, `note` |
| **C7** (Check the book only) | Per record: `history` claims and `detail:*` claims whose values disagree and that are about one object (same non-null `object_id`) or share at least two content words of four or more letters (`queryTerms` over the values, the record's own names left out); at most 5 pairs per claim, nearest in reading order first (P4-D9). | two `claim`s |

The judge's **per-reading limit** (design: "≤10 pairs per reading") is slice 2's; generation here is complete.

### 1.6 The test book (`src/main/memory/testBook/`, test data only)

The book already labels three contradictions (C1 Varrow s03/h09, C2 Hobb h03/h09, C3 Bel h04/h05) and finding negatives (the h06 dream, Dunmore's own speech). ### 2.2 `judge.v1` (fast tier, JSON, reasoning off; input cap 3,000; at most 10 pairs per call)

Opening (the e2e fake's sentinel): "You are the contradiction judge inside a novel-writing app."

| Part (in prompt order) | Budget | Cached |
|---|---|---|
| System: rules and schema (below) | ≤ 450 (golden asserts) | yes |
| User: "Pairs:" then up to 10 items, each ≤ 260 tokens: the question (by rule, order-neutral), then A and B, each `[ref ¶p · earlier \| later \| same scene \| note · voice, mode · flashback · story year Y]` and its text (the quote's sentence ±1, cut around the quote to 450 characters by `clipAround`; a note sentence ±1 within its paragraph), then "Between:" up to two time-skip quotes (`elapsedBreaks` hints between the scenes) | ≤ 2,500 | no |

Item questions (order-neutral, so the swapped call reads true): C1 "Mara Vell · eyes. Can both be true?"; C2 dead "One passage has Hobb die; the other has him present. Can both be true?"; C2 body "Mara's hair differs between these two appearances with no change shown. Can both be true?"; C3 "In one passage Bel passes on the secret engagement; in the other she learns it. Can both be true?"; C4 birth "Stated age 17; born 1795; the story year of that scene is 1815 (typed by the author). Can both be true?"; C4 falls "Lottie's stated age falls from one passage to the later one. Can both be true?"; C5 "One passage resolves Lottie's match (a plot thread); the other, later, advances it. Can both be true?"; C6 "One passage is the book, the other the author's note. Can both be true of Orrin's eyes?"; C7 "The Captain · history. Can both be true?".

**Rules** (compact; the golden asserts the total):
- Each numbered item is a pair of passages from one novel or from the author's notes, with what the app found that may not fit. Decide whether both can be true in the story as written: `c` they cannot both be true at their places in the story; `ok` both can be true (a change the story shows or allows over the time between them, a different person or thing, a figure of speech, a character lying, guessing or mistaken, a rumour, a memory, a dream, a hypothetical); `u` these words do not settle it.
- Reading order is story order, except in a flashback or a remembered moment.
- Notes are the author's timeless canon: they describe the whole story and may describe the future, so a note about what happens later is not contradicted by an earlier scene. A fixed fact in a note (eyes, birth, family) must match the book.
- Narration states what is true in the story; a character's words, thoughts or rumours can be wrong on purpose only where the passage shows it.
- Judge only from the words given.
- Reply `{"v":[{"i":1,"r":"c","w":"grey eyes against brown"}]}`: one entry per item, in order; `w` (at most 8 words) only with `c`. Nothing else.

**Two orders, two calls** (design §2.4, P4-D11): a batch is sent once with each item as A,B and once, as a separate request, with every item as B,A (the passages and their labels swapped, the question unchanged), so the second verdict is not the first one echoed. Each pair-order is cached on its own.

**Caps:** `judgeMaxTokens(n) = min(300, 20 + 26 × n)`. A reply cut off at the cap is parsed up to its last complete item (`salvageJsonItems`, P2a's); the unanswered items stay unjudged for the next run and are counted. **Pro confirm** (slice 3) is the same version with a single item at ±3 sentences each side (1,200 characters), ≤ 1,000 tokens in, `{"r":"c","w":"…"}` with `w` at most 20 words for every verdict, `max_tokens` 90, tier `strong`, reasoning off.

### 2.3 Judging (`src/main/memory/findings/judge.ts`, `findingsService.ts`)

1. **Pair items** (`renderPairItem(finding, inputs)`): sides resolved against the book as it is now (the claim's quote located in its paragraph; a gone side drops the item, and the next recompute closes the finding).
2. **Cache key** (`verdictKey(item, order, tier)`): a hash of the prompt version, the tier, the order and the rendered item with scene numbers and paragraph numbers masked and every name or nickname folded to its record token (`gateText`'s folding), so a scene move or a Rename in book costs no new verdict, while a changed word, context sentence, relative order, voice, mode, flashback flag, story year or between-hint does (design §2.2's `verdict_cache` key: quotes, context sentences, relative order, modes and flashback).
3. **Batches** (`judgeBatches(items)`): at most 10 items and 2,500 tokens of items per call; the A,B call and the B,A call of one batch share its items. Pairs already cached in one order send only the other.
4. **Request** through `runAiRequest` (feature `judge`, tier `fast`, `json: true`; the request path's own context-hash cache also applies); each call's cost goes to `onSpend` and is shared out to its findings' `cost_usd`.
5. **Verdicts:** `parseJudgeAnswer` (lenient: unknown `r` is no verdict; `w` cut to 8 words); each pair-order cached; `verdictOf` sets the state (`open`, `unclear`, `fine`, or still `unjudged` with one order missing).
6. **Background runs** (the `judge` job, P4-D12): queued by the `findings` job when unjudged candidates exist and the switch allows; at most 10 pairs a run (2 calls), the scene just read first, then `RULE_ORDER`; the job queues itself again while unjudged pairs remain, up to 30 pairs a day in the background; the rest wait for Check the book (slice 3's `findingsWaiting` notice). No background run while a rebuild runs (its sweep is slice 3's). Gates before any request: Use AI and the **Contradiction check** switch, a provider, the daily cap, and the Cloud monthly cap for background spend (P2a-D9); a hold throws `JobSkippedError` (quiet, nothing retried in a loop). The reader's health gate does not apply.
7. Push `memory:findingsChanged` and `memory:changed` for the records whose findings moved.

## 8. Decisions this plan makes (decided by Claude, unconfirmed; logged in `QUESTIONS.md`)

- **P4-D1.** "Claims that are not narration in `now` mode never raise C1, C2 or C6 on their own" (design §2.4) is read as: a C1 pair needs at least one narration-now side; C2's death claim must be narration-now; a C6 pair has the note as its canon side, so the book side may be speech, rumour or a memory (which is why design §2.4 withholds *Update my note* for those). The judge sees voice and mode and decides.
- **P4-D2.** C1 groups agreeing values (equal or contained, `detailsAgree`), takes the earliest narration-now claim of each group as its representative, and pairs every other group with the earliest group that has one: k groups, k−1 pairs.
- **P4-D3.** C2 `dead` needs an on-page presence with its verified quote that still stands; a typed POV or *+ also here* never raises it. Death is read from a closed word list; a later narration-now "alive" value between the two cancels it.
- **P4-D4.** C2 `body` covers hair and scars, between a record's adjacent appearances (from the stored cards and typed details, so no text is read), and is cancelled by any claim of that slot from A to B that states a change.
- **P4-D5.** Group membership (P2b left it to P4): a character belongs to a group when a narration claim relates them to it, a lasting claim (role, allegiance, rank, occupation, home) names it, or a canon Pinned line on either sheet names the other. Used only to skip C3 (so it can only remove a candidate).
- **P4-D6.** C4 counts only a year typed for that scene or quoted (and verified) in it, never a carried one; both years must be in the same calendar (the same words beside the digits); one year either way is allowed.
- **P4-D7.** C5 pairs a resolve with a later `advances` step only (the design's word); a thread re-opened with a new `opens` step is not a candidate.
- **P4-D8.** C6 pairs a note sentence that names the record (or sits on its own Pinned page) and holds a slot cue; its value must be readable in code (a `Label: value` line or a literal pattern), else it is not compared; kinship also needs the object named; dated lines resolve their scene by title, then number. At most 2 sentences per claim.
- **P4-D9.** C7 pairs history and detail claims about one object or sharing two content words of four or more letters (the record's names left out), at most 5 per claim, nearest first; Check the book only.
- **P4-D10.** Verdicts are `c`, `ok`, `u`. Two `c` make a finding; any `ok` clears it; otherwise it is unclear: listed muted under Conflicts and in the check's report, never in the badge, never underlined. Pro's `ok` hides an open finding (listed in the report); Pro's `u` makes it unclear.
- **P4-D11.** The two orders go in two separate calls of the same batch, so the second verdict is independent of the first; each pair-order is cached on its own.
- **P4-D12.** Background judging: at most 10 pairs per run (2 calls), the scene just read first, at most 30 pairs a day; never during a rebuild, which ends with one asked sweep of up to 60 pairs; what is left waits for Check the book, with a priced notice.
- **P4-D13.** A candidate with a stale side is held (kept, not judged, not shown) and never closes for being stale; a flashback-dependent candidate is hidden and reported by Check the book with *Not a flashback?*, which marks the card's flashback not right.
- **P4-D14.** Findings are keyed by their rule and the *Not a problem* key (record, slot, both values and both places, sorted), so a re-read or a revived claim keeps the same finding; a note's place is its owner (`note:record:<id>` or `note:research:<id>`). *Same as…* re-keys them to the kept record.
- **P4-D15.** Underlines: at most 3 marks a scene (a same-scene pair is two; a cross-scene finding marks its quote in each scene), by rule order then newest; only open findings; never in a paragraph with an unread finished sentence or the caret's paragraph; a paragraph edited since the last refresh loses its marks until main redraws them. A note side is never underlined (notes are pages, not the manuscript); the book quote's hover card shows it.
- **P4-D16.** Underline precision is counted over the last 20 shown findings that closed: dismissed as *Not a problem* counts against, *This one's right*, *Update my note* or the text changing counts for; the question is asked once, and the choice lives in Settings (`reading.underlines`) where it can be changed back.
- **P4-D17.** *This one's right* marks the other side not right (a claim's *Wrong value*, a presence's card *Not right*) and never edits the manuscript; on a note conflict the manuscript is canon (answer 1), so the panel offers *Update my note* instead, and on C4's year side nothing.
- **P4-D18.** *Update my note* is a code-made proposal (the note's value words replaced by the book's), edited and saved by the author; it is offered only when the book side is narration in `now` mode (stricter than the design's "not speech, rumour or a memory": thought, dream and hypothetical are left out too).
- **P4-D19.** Loose ends: the window is max(8 scenes, a quarter of the readable scenes); threads are measured up to the earlier of the furthest drafted scene and "checked up to"; characters must be named in at least 3 scenes and not dead; planned cards must sit before the furthest drafted scene with no link; each has *Not a problem* (it comes back if the thing is touched again later); at most 10 shown.
- **P4-D20.** Needs a look's order: My to-dos on top (design §2.7), then Conflicts, Questions, Loose ends, Notices; the badge counts conflicts only (filed lines, typed details, open findings).
- **P4-D21.** The **Contradiction check** switch off hides findings and underlines and judges nothing; with Use AI off, judged findings stay visible ("judging paused"), as claims do, and the local parts (the recompute, closing, loose ends) keep working.
- **P4-D22.** Check the book shows "about" and "at most" prices (Pro calls depend on what the fast check finds) and "free" when nothing is uncached; its report is kept (`memoryCheck`) until the next check.
- **P4-D23.** In v2, "Check this scene for consistency" (route or quick action) answers locally with the open scene's open findings and *Check the book*; v1 keeps `continuity.v2`.

## 9. Gates, ticks, docs, and what P4 does not do

- **Gates per slice:** typecheck, lint per folder, test, test:e2e (`memory.spec.ts` and the unedited smoke), eval:ai. Slice 2 runs `eval:ai -- -u` and quotes the token-report diff. No live run is possible here; the live contradictions run and live fidelity are built for the author (§6.2).
- **Ticks:** none in slices 1–5 (progress notes in the F-16.27–16.30 text); all four after slice 6's final verification, unless the run's instructions say not to tick.
- **Docs in the same commits:** `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md` (one bullet per slice), `docs/AI-COST-REGISTRY.md` (slice 2), `QUESTIONS.md` (each slice's calls), `FEATURES.md` progress notes, `CLAUDE.md` "Next up" and the tier rule (slices 2 and 6), `docs/OPERATOR-SETUP.md` (slice 6).
- **Not in P4:**
  - travel times, distances and where objects are (no rule covers them; Check the book says so);
  - the opt-in deep check (F-16.37, P8) and local-model calibration of the judge (F-16.36);
  - Ms Scribe answering from findings beyond the contested instant card and the local continuity answer;
  - contradictions inside Try chats and Versions (P5: never read into memory);
  - deleting `continuity.v2`, the Continuity view and `continuity_finding` (the switch-over, F-16.31; the table goes in P9).
- **Risks:**
  - **False underlines** (design Part 6, risk 4): two independent verdicts, at most 3 per scene, closing on their own, and the one question at 70 % bound them; the live run measures precision before the switch-over.
  - **Few candidates** where the reader is thin (design risk 1): a missed claim means a missed contradiction, never a false one; *Check the book* says what it did not check.
  - **The recompute on every change** must stay under 50 ms (a perf test holds it); if the book grows past that, the job recomputes only the records a change touched.
  - **Note sentences in free prose** are compared only when code can read their value, so many notes are never checked against the book. The live run lists the note conflicts it found; a later version could let the judge read unparsed note sentences, at a cost.
  - **Slice 3 is the largest.** If it runs long, *Update my note* and the loose-end channels move to slice 4 with their UI, and slice 3 keeps Check the book, the finding actions and the instant card.
