How it works

You write. It keeps the notes.

Write the way you always have. As you save each scene, MythScribe reads it and files what it finds, so the things you used to stop for are already done.

No more looking things up

Ask your book anything.

Where was she last seen? What does the reader know by chapter ten? Does this line sound like him? Ask in plain words and get the answer with the passage it came from. If it isn't in your book, it says so.

No more bookkeeping

Your story bible fills itself in.

Every character, place, and rule of your world gets a sheet. What each scene says about them (an age, a scar, who was where) lands on its sheet with the line it came from, and every nickname and title points to the same person. Already have notes? Upload them and it sorts them into sheets, asking before anything lands.

  • No more lost threads

    Blue eyes in chapter two, grey in chapter twenty? When a scene disagrees with your notes or an earlier scene, it tells you where, quoting both, and waits until you look.

  • Everything else a novel needs

    A binder for parts, chapters, and scenes, a quiet full-screen page, drafts, snapshots, and backups, and compile to a print-ready PDF, EPUB, or Word.

  • AI is optional

    Write with it off, use your own key, or run a model on your own computer. Your book stays on your computer, and nothing goes into it unless you let it.
