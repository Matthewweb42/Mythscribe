What does your own OpenAI key cost?

A rough estimate, not a quote: about $6 a month for a writer who adds 1,000 words a day, keeps background indexing on, takes about 20 VibeWrite suggestions a day, and asks the assistant 3 questions a day, with an OpenAI key on the app’s OpenAI models (GPT-5.4 mini for background work and suggestions, GPT-5.4 for the assistant) at OpenAI’s prices of 5 October 2026. The OpenRouter defaults (DeepSeek V4 Flash and Pro) are priced lower per token.

Rough monthly cost with your own OpenAI key
What Assumption Rough cost
Background indexing Summaries, story bible, and tags for about 30,000 new words about $0.60 a month
VibeWrite About 20 suggestions a day about $0.80 a month
Assistant About 3 questions a day, each with a few lookups about $4.60 a month
A line edit One pass over a 90,000-word novel, when you run it up to about $2.50 each time

Your own numbers depend on how you write and what you ask. The app shows the cost of every request and a running total, the edit-pass screen shows an estimate before you start, and the daily cap (set to $2 when you install) stops requests once it is reached. A local model costs nothing.
